# REAmo Roadmap

Where REAmo is heading, in broad strokes. No dates and no promised order;
this page changes when something ships or a theme is dropped.

## Instruments

- **Drum pads:** edit pads, save kits, note repeat.
- **Piano:** scale lock and glissando.
- **Chord pads:** custom chord banks and richer chord types.

## Recording and editing

- **Swipe comping on the phone:** build a comp from your takes without going back to the computer.
- **Item moves that follow REAPER:** ripple, snap and grouping respected.
- **Automation:** switch modes, record fader moves, see curves.
- **MIDI note editing.**
- **Take FX and FX containers.**

## Playing and practising

- **Setlists:** a Playlist mode for rehearsal and gigs.
- **Practice metronome:** gap click and speed trainer.
- **Chord charts and lyrics:** paste a chart, read it on stage, with a teleprompter that follows the song.
- **Listen with the phone locked:** Mix Monitoring that keeps playing in your pocket.

## Everyday use

- **Find REAmo by name** on your network, without typing an address.
- **Themes,** including high contrast, and an accessibility pass.
- **Rock-solid basics:** Scrap/Retake/Keep under every edge case, reconnecting over WiFi and USB tethering, clear error messages when something isn't set up, and a visual polish pass.
